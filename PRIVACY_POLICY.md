Privacy Policy – Isaac’s Invoicing Pro

Effective date: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

Isaac’s Invoicing Pro is an invoicing and business management application designed to help users manage customers, invoices, payments, inventory, expenses and financial reports.

2. Information Processed

The application may process business names, customer contact details, invoice information, payment records, inventory records and expenses entered by users.

3. Data Storage

The current web-based application stores business information locally in the user’s browser or device storage. This information is not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices and maintaining secure backups.

4. Data Sharing

Isaac’s Invoicing Pro does not sell customer or business information to advertisers.

Information may be included in documents or backups that users choose to export or share.

5. Data Security

Users should protect their devices, maintain backups and avoid sharing sensitive financial records with unauthorized persons.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on the user’s device or browser until deleted through the application or browser storage controls. Removing browser data may permanently erase records without a backup.

7. Children’s Privacy

The application is intended for business use and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application features or data-handling practices change.
